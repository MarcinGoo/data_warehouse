# Business Case & Actionable Insights

## 1. Business Problem & Goal

The Challenge:
The company was operating in a highly fragmented data landscape. Sales data from Amazon (B2C) and international wholesale (B2B) were stored in disjointed CSV files. The lack of a centralized Single Source of Truth made it impossible to accurately track overall revenue, monitor fulfillment cancellation rates, or identify bloated inventory (frozen capital).

The Goal:
To design and build an automated Data Warehouse and Business Intelligence reporting suite that unifies global sales channels, cleanses the data, and provides decision-makers with Actionable Insights to drive revenue growth, reduce logistics losses, and optimize warehouse space.

## 2. What We Achieved

We successfully engineered a full-stack data platform and delivered three business dashboards in Power BI:

- Executive Summary: High-level view of growth, net revenue, and category performance.
- Operations and Fulfillment: Deep-dive into logistics, specifically tracking Lost Revenue due to order cancellations and returns.
- Inventory Health: A highly actionable dashboard identifying Top 10 Dead Stock SKUs for marketing clearance, and High-Risk Fast Movers for immediate procurement.

## 3. Business Conclusions & Value

By deploying this platform, the business can now draw the following immediate conclusions and take action:

- Inventory Optimization: By replacing scattered Excel sheets with the Inventory Health Dashboard, category managers can instantly identify which products are tying up capital (Dead Stock) and liquidate them, while preventing stockouts of best-sellers.
- Logistics Accountability: The explicit tracking of cancelled amounts in the Fact table allows the operations team to pinpoint exactly which product categories or regions suffer the highest cancellation/return rates, enabling root-cause analysis.
- Strategic Omnichannel View: Executives can finally compare B2B (Wholesale) and B2C (Amazon) margins and sales volumes side-by-side in a unified data model, enabling better budget allocation.
